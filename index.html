An open source organization

Open source, built for people.

Neyam OpenSc builds free software for the people the technology industry tends to overlook — people who speak languages it doesn't prioritize, on phones it no longer optimizes for, in places its cloud doesn't reach. Everything we make is open source, private by default, and free forever.

What we believe

  1. People over platforms

    Software should serve the person holding the phone — not an engagement metric, an advertiser, or a data pipeline.

  2. Open by default

    Code, documentation, decisions and roadmaps are public from day one. Apache-2.0 licensed, community governed.

  3. Privacy is not a setting

    Our software doesn't send your data anywhere, because it isn't built to. No accounts, no telemetry, no ads — by architecture.

  4. Kind software

    No dark patterns, no manufactured urgency, no noise. We call it Tender UX, and we write it down so it can't quietly erode.
